About the lab

Welcome to the GeoSENSE Lab, based in the Environmental Health, Climate, and Sustainability program at the School of Public Health, LSU Health New Orleans.

We are an interdisciplinary team working where earth observation meets public health. By combining satellite imagery, geospatial data, health data, and GeoAI, we build exposure maps and risk assessments that help public health agencies, planners, and communities prepare for a changing climate. Our home on the Gulf Coast shapes our questions: extreme heat, poor air quality, flooding, and disaster risk are daily realities here.

Explore our research, projects, and capabilities, meet our people, catch up on news, or get in touch to join or collaborate with us.

Our research has been supported by NASA, NIH, NSF, NOAA, USAID, and the Wellcome Trust.

Capabilities

What we bring to collaborations and funded projects.

Earth observation

  • Landsat, Sentinel-1/2, MODIS, and NASA TEMPO data
  • Multi-temporal image processing and compositing
  • Land surface temperature retrieval

GeoAI

  • Machine learning for land cover and environmental mapping
  • Deep learning for satellite and aerial image analysis
  • Spatiotemporal modeling of environmental exposures
  • Large-scale processing on high-performance computing

GIS and spatial analysis

  • Vulnerability and risk indices
  • Accessibility and network analysis

Environmental epidemiology

  • Exposure assessment for health studies
  • Linking environmental and health data
  • Community-focused risk communication

Decision-support tools

  • Interactive web maps for research communication
  • Web tools for exploring exposure and risk data
