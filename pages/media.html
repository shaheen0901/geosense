Jan 222025

New study maps the 'dental deserts' in the U.S.

Harvard Gazette

The Harvard Gazette covered our JAMA Network Open study mapping spatial access to dental clinics across the U.S. at the census block group level, which found about 1.7 million people living in dental deserts. Dr. Rahman is quoted on how the approach identifies underserved areas missed by earlier studies.
